LanternTail Companion

A chibi fennec companion with a crystal tail that really lights the way. Two color variants — the purple Girl and orange Boy — share one optimized rig, sixteen hand-tuned animations, and playable demo scenes in both engines. Drop her in, press play, and she walks, hops, jumps, sits, and celebrates — with her lantern glowing neon or warm.
